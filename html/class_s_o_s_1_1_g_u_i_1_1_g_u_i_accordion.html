Detailed Description

A collapsible container with an animated chevron icon and a content area.

Consists of a header button with a dropdown-style chevron icon and a content area (GUILayoutBuilder) that can be shown/hidden. The chevron rotates -90 degrees when collapsed. Toggling triggers layout recalculation on the accordion and its parent layout group.
